# Notifications and integrations

Where cases meet the organization's own workflow. This is bidirectional case workflow, distinct
from forwarding telemetry to a SIEM.

## Operator notifications (built)

A tenant admin registers a signed HTTPS webhook at `/console/notifications` (API:
`/api/notify/channels`). The events `host.degraded`, `host.silent`, `host.recovered` and
`hunt.new_matches` go through an outbox and are sent by the `deliver-notifications` scheduled job,
at least once. A message points to the console and carries no detection contents. The server
needs `NOTIFY_SECRET_KEY` to store channel secrets; see
[README-SETUP.md](../README-SETUP.md#notifications).

A receiver must:

- **Check the signature.** `X-Synthaea-Signature: t=<unix>,v1=<hex>` carries an HMAC-SHA256 of
  `<t>.<body>` with the channel secret. Enforce a window of about 5 minutes on `t`; every attempt
  is signed again with a fresh `t`. For 24 hours after a secret rotation the header carries two
  values, `v1=<new>,v1=<old>`: accept either.
- **De-duplicate and order.** Drop an event whose `id` was already applied, and apply an event only
  if its `seq` is greater than the last one applied for that `subject`. Delivery is at least once,
  and a failed message does not block later ones.
- **Treat text as untrusted.** A hunt's name and a host's name are chosen by people or by the
  host, not by the platform: escape them before rendering them in a chat message, a ticket or a
  page. The identifier fields (`id`, `tenant`, `subject.id`, `seq`) are generated by the platform.
- **Answer quickly.** A `2xx` within 10 seconds is success; the body is not read.

Delivery: `408`, `425`, `429`, `5xx` and network errors are retried after 1, 5 and 30 minutes,
then 2 and 6 hours, honoring `Retry-After` on `429` and `503`. A `410` pauses the channel; any
other answer is final. Five failed messages in a row pause the channel; its messages are kept and
sent, oldest first, when an admin re-enables it. Delivered messages are kept 30 days, failed ones
90.

Code: `lib/notify/`, `app/api/notify/`, `app/console/notifications/`. Tests:
`tests/integration/notify-*.test.ts`.

## Planned

- **Ticketing.** Cases open and update Jira or ServiceNow issues with two-way status sync.
- **Paging.** Severity-threshold routing to PagerDuty, Opsgenie or Slack, de-duplicated by case: a
  case pages once, not once per detection.
- **Case webhooks and inbound actions.** Signed outbound webhooks on case lifecycle events and a
  small inbound API (acknowledge, annotate, trigger an existing policy-gated playbook).
  Integrations get no capability the console does not have, and every inbound action is
  authenticated and audited like an analyst's.
